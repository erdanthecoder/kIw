What is CouchPlay?

CouchPlay is a couch-gaming platform born from a simple idea: everyone already owns a game console — they just don't know it. A TV plus the phones in everyone's pockets is all the hardware a great game night needs.


Open the console page on a big screen and it becomes a PlayStation-style home with a room code. Friends join in seconds from their phone browser. The host picks a game, every phone transforms into the right controller — a joystick with the classic shape buttons, a quiz pad, a drawing canvas, or a full first-person 3D view — and the living room comes alive. Connections run peer-to-peer straight between devices, so it feels instant, and a relay server steps in automatically on strict networks.

How it works

1

Open the console

Put console.html on your TV, laptop or projector. It shows a 4-letter room code and a QR code.

2

Friends join

Everyone scans the QR or types the code on their phone. Up to 8 players — phones, tablets and laptops all work.

3

Pick and play

The host picks from 16 games. Every phone morphs into the right controller automatically. The leaderboard tracks the session's champion.
